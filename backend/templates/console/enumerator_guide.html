Enumerator quick guide

{{ SITE_NAME }} — one page to keep with you in the field.

smartphoneWhat you do

You collect data on your phone in ODK Collect. It syncs to {{ SITE_NAME }}, where your coordinator reviews it. You don't need the website in the field — only to check your work and corrections.

downloadBefore you go out

  1. Install ODK Collect from the Play Store.
  2. Get the server QR code or URL from your coordinator (Manage → Forms → Overview shows it).
  3. In ODK Collect: Configure via QR code, sign in, then Get Blank Form.
  4. Download the forms you'll use so you can work offline.

edit_noteCollecting — every record

  • One submission per plot / farmer / household.
  • Enter your enumerator ID (ENID) exactly — it links the data to you.
  • Scan the barcode / sample ID — don't type it (typos cause duplicates & mismatches).
  • Take the required photo(s); keep GPS/location on at the plot.
  • Finalize, then Send when you have signal (you can send later in batches).

fact_checkCheck your work

On {{ SITE_NAME }} open My submissions after a sync. You'll see each record and any issues to fix.

buildIf something is flagged

You'll get an email listing what to fix, and it shows on My submissions. Correct it in ODK Collect and re-send. Common flags:
  • Duplicate sample ID — two records share one barcode.
  • Missing photo — an expected attachment wasn't uploaded.
  • Value out of range — a number looks wrong (typo / unit).
  • GPS far from the plot — wrong plot, or location was off.
  • Unknown ID — the sample isn't in the planned list.

Stuck? Contact your project coordinator. Keep this sheet in your bag.
